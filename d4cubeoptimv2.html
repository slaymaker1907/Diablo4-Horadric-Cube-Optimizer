        How To Use

        1. Enter current affixes and mark GA and Enchanted flags where applicable.
        2. Enter desired target affixes. Require GA is only available for affixes that are already GA on the source item.
        3. Set Legendary if removal is unavailable for your item.
        4. Choose thinking time. Use 10s for quick guidance, or 0 for unlimited search and click Stop when satisfied.
        5. Click Optimize and follow the recommended operation.
        6. After each in-game outcome, use Apply Last Outcome to update state and re-run optimization.
        7. Target GA requirements are enforced strictly; the optimizer will reject impossible GA targets instead of estimating a success chance.
        8. Use Reset to clear local state and restart from scratch.
        Current Limitations
        • Affix pool is derived from prism categories only, not slot-accurate roll tables.
        • Gear slot is stored and shown for future extension, but does not yet filter affix pools.
        • Affix values or thresholds are not modeled yet (example: minimum 1500 Maximum Life).
        • Gold and crafting materials are not included in objective cost.
        • MCTS results are estimates and improve with more compute time.
